<section class="sc-sec" style="padding-top:40px">
<div class="sc-head">
<h2 class="sc-kicker">Across the life of a study</h2>
<span>Join at any point; earlier is usually better</span>
</div>
<div class="sc-grid5">
<div>
<div class="sc-stage">Design</div>
<p class="sc-body">Framing aims as models, choosing waves and measures, and sizing the sample before anything is collected.</p>
</div>
<div>
<div class="sc-stage">Measurement</div>
<p class="sc-body">Establishing that a scale holds up - factor structure, reliability, and invariance across groups and time.</p>
</div>
<div>
<div class="sc-stage">Analysis</div>
<p class="sc-body">Fitting the model the question calls for, with the missing data, nesting, and attrition handled honestly.</p>
</div>
<div>
<div class="sc-stage">Writing</div>
<p class="sc-body">Methods and results sections, tables and figures, and replies to the reviewer who questions the approach.</p>
</div>
<div>
<div class="sc-stage">Funding</div>
<p class="sc-body">Analysis plans, power, and budget language for the next proposal - often the same study, one stage on.</p>
</div>
</div>
</section>

<section class="sc-sec sc-sec-top" id="methods">
<div class="sc-head">
<h2 class="sc-kicker">Methods</h2>
<span>Grouped by the question they answer - you do not need to know which one you need</span>
</div>
<div style="border-top:2px solid var(--div)">
<div class="sc-fam">
<h3>How does something change over time?</h3>
<p>Latent growth curve models, multilevel modeling, latent transition analysis, and repeated-measures designs.</p>
</div>
<div class="sc-fam">
<h3>Are there distinct subgroups in the sample?</h3>
<p>Latent class analysis, latent class growth analysis, growth mixture modeling, and profile comparison.</p>
</div>
<div class="sc-fam">
<h3>Is the measure capturing the construct?</h3>
<p>Confirmatory factor analysis, item response theory, measurement invariance, and scale development.</p>
</div>
<div class="sc-fam">
<h3>What connects one variable to another?</h3>
<p>Structural equation modeling, path analysis, mediation and moderation, and regression.</p>
</div>
<div class="sc-fam">
<h3>When does an event occur?</h3>
<p>Discrete-time and continuous-time survival analysis, with time-varying covariates.</p>
</div>
</div>
<p style="font-size:15px; line-height:1.6; margin-top:22px; max-width:82ch; color:var(--n800)">Analyses are run in <a href="https://www.statmodel.com/">Mplus</a>, <a href="https://cran.r-project.org/">R</a>, <a href="https://www.sas.com/en_us/home.html">SAS</a>, <a href="https://www.stata.com/">Stata</a>, <a href="https://www.ibm.com/spss">SPSS</a>, or <a href="https://ssicentral.com/index.php/products/lisrel/">LISREL</a> - whichever your team already works in - and you get the annotated syntax back with the results.</p>
</section>
